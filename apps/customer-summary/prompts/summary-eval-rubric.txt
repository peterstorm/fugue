Evaluate the customer summary output against these criteria:

- Factuality: Every claim in the summary (sentiment, topics, actions, risk level) must be directly traceable to the input conversation data. No hallucinated details, invented customer statements, or fabricated context. Score 0.0 if any claim cannot be found in the source material.

- Completeness: The summary must cover all key dimensions: sentiment assessment, main topics discussed, interaction summary, action items, risk level, and satisfaction. Missing a required dimension scores 0.0. Partial coverage scores proportionally.

- Relevance: The summary must focus on information useful to a support team. No off-topic content, no generic filler text, no restating obvious metadata. Every sentence should add actionable insight.

- Coherence: The summary must read as a unified analysis, not disconnected bullet points. Assessments (sentiment, risk, satisfaction) should be consistent with each other and with the evidence cited.

Score each criterion 0.0-1.0. A criterion fails below 0.8.
